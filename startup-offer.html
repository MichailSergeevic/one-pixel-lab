    How this decision actually gets made
    1. Every offer becomes three numbers on a 0–1 scale: salary, equity, and company size (size is on a log scale, so 8 vs 15 people matters more than 150 vs 157).
    2. The classifier measures the distance from your offer to every labeled example. Smaller distance means more similar.
    3. The 3 closest examples vote. If the vote is split, the single closest example wins.
    4. If even the closest example is far away, it says "Not sure" instead of guessing. Teaching it a similar example fixes that.

    It has no rule like "salary over 70K is high." The only thing it knows is the examples below, so different examples give different answers.
